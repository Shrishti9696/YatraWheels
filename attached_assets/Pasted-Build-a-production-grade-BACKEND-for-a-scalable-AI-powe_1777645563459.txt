PROJECT VISION:

YatraWheels is a platform where users can:

* Book vehicles (cars, vans, buses) for trips
* Arrange group transport for destination weddings/events
* Get AI-generated trip plans
* Make secure payments for bookings
* Manage bookings and travel routes

This is a vehicle booking + travel platform (NOT a wedding planner).

DATABASE DESIGN (PRODUCTION LEVEL):

Use MongoDB with optimized schemas, validation, and indexing.

1. USER MODEL:

* name (string, required)
* email (string, unique, indexed)
* password (hashed)
* role (user/admin)
* createdAt

Validation:

* Email format validation
* Required fields

---

2. VEHICLE MODEL:

* type (car | van | bus)
* capacity (number)
* pricePerKm (number)
* location (string)
* features (array of strings)
* isAvailable (boolean)
* createdAt

Indexes:

* location
* type
* pricePerKm

---

3. BOOKING MODEL:

* userId (ObjectId, ref: User, indexed)
* vehicleId (ObjectId, ref: Vehicle, indexed)
* pickupLocation
* dropLocation
* date (indexed)
* passengers
* totalPrice
* status (pending | confirmed | cancelled)
* createdAt

CRITICAL BUSINESS LOGIC:

* Prevent double booking (same vehicle, same date)
* Validate passengers <= vehicle capacity

---

4. TRIP MODEL:

* userId (ref: User)
* destination

* itinerary (array of objects)
* createdAt

5. PAYMENT MODEL:
