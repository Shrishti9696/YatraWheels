Design and build a full-scale AI-powered marketplace platform called "YatraWheels" with FAANG-level UI/UX, complete frontend + backend architecture, and production-grade workflows.

---

CORE PRODUCT VISION:

YatraWheels is an AI-powered travel and vehicle marketplace platform with:

1. Users → Book vehicles and plan trips using AI
2. Vendors (Vehicle Owners) → List and manage vehicles
3. Drivers → Provide driving services
4. Admin → Control platform, analytics, and payments

The platform should feel like a real-world product similar to Uber or Airbnb, combined with modern AI SaaS experiences.

---

* Style: Glassmorphism + soft shadows
* Border radius: 16–24px

* Clean spacing and alignment
* Mobile-first responsive design

8. PAYMENT SYSTEM (FULLY AUTOMATED)

### Flow:

* User pays full amount to platform
* Payment is verified via webhook
* Money is held (escrow-style)
* Trip lifecycle:
  pending → confirmed → ongoing → completed

### On trip completion:

* Automatically trigger payouts:

  * Vendor receives vendorAmount
  * Driver receives driverAmount
  * Platform keeps commission

### Pricing logic:

Total =
vehicle price

* driver fee
* distance cost
* platform fee
